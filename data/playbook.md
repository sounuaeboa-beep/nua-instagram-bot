# nu.a content playbook

**Positioning:** Affordable Everyday Essentials.
**Objective:** attractive, relatable, conversion-oriented content that emphasizes comfort, quality, practicality and accessible pricing.

## Content mix (enforced by the rotation in `content.py`)
- 40% everyday comfort and lifestyle
- 25% product education
- 25% multipacks and conversion
- 10% aspirational visual content

## Design
Clean, modern, feminine, natural lighting, real-looking models, accurate product details, uncluttered compositions.

## Voice
Friendly, confident, straightforward Brazilian Portuguese. Emphasize smart purchases and daily comfort.

## Reference brands (what to learn, by inspiration weight)
1. **DeMillus (30%)** — everyday underwear positioning, relatable women, practical comfort benefits, straightforward product presentation, accessible commercial messaging, educational Reels.
2. **Lupo / Loba (25%)** — product functionality, microfiber benefits, seamless design, fabric texture, detailed close-ups, fit demonstrations, simple informative content.
3. **DelRio (20%)** — multipack storytelling, value for money, bundle offers, persuasive carousels, promotional hooks, clear purchase-oriented CTAs.
4. **HOPE (15%)** — clean photography, modern lifestyle imagery, approachable femininity, polished identity, natural poses, elevated product presentation.
5. **Intimissimi (10%)** — minimalist art direction, sophisticated lighting, elegant compositions, premium typography, visual consistency. Avoid luxury pricing cues and overly sensual positioning.

## Reference rule
Learn hooks, composition, storytelling and formats from competitors. Never reproduce their photographs, branding, captions or campaign assets.

## Observed patterns
Source: the 12 most recent posts of each profile, read on 2026-11-09 (captions, formats, profile info). Visual composition was
not analysed in depth. Learn the patterns below; never copy captions, photos or campaign names.

**Format mix of the last 12 posts** (reels vs. photo/carousel posts): DeMillus 6/6, Lupo 9/3, DelRio 3/9, HOPE 5/7,
Intimissimi 5/7. Reels are about half of everything big brands post; ours are photo posts for now.

**DeMillus (30%)**
- Hook = a plain, relatable statement or question about comfort ("a calcinha boa é a que você veste e não quer mais trocar").
- Then the real product detail: fabric (microfibra), seamless cut, what it does for the day. Close with an easy question ("Já escolheu a sua?").
- Tells the product's story when it has one (heritage, a famous test). Sign-off tagline repeats on many posts. No prices in captions.

**Lupo / Loba (25%)**
- Short, benefit-first copy: texture, softness, resistance, freedom of movement. One clear CTA: where to buy ("link na bio").
- Heavy on seasonal themes and creator collaborations marked as paid partnership. Few emojis, calm tone.

**DelRio (20%)**
- The multipack and promo reference. Offers are in nearly every caption (percent off, progressive "leve mais, pague menos"), plus box
  bundles ("7 calcinhas em uma caixa").
- Curiosity hooks with a 👀, and engagement prompts ("qual é a sua favorita?", "comente ISSO ou AQUILO"). Clear arrow CTA to the site.
- For nu.a: use the bundle storytelling and a clear CTA, but only real kit prices from the catalog. No invented discounts, no fake urgency.

**HOPE (15%)**
- Very short captions (1 to 3 lines), polished and warm, always ending with the same brand hashtag. Named collections, "sem marcar" as the benefit.
- Uses "comment a keyword and we send the link in DM": we cannot automate that, so use "link na bio" instead.

**Intimissimi (10%)**
- One product line shown again and again as a series (the invisible bra line, six of twelve posts). Consistent, recognisable, simple caption template.
- Product codes in captions. Skip the luxury vocabulary (pure silk, luxury) and the sensual angle.

## nu.a rules derived from the study
1. Hook in the first line: a relatable everyday moment or a short question. Max one emoji in the hook.
2. Name one concrete product fact from the catalog (cut, fabric, colours), not generic praise.
3. Multipack days: say what the kit saves per piece, with the real numbers. Close with "link na bio".
4. A small branded hashtag set every time (#nua plus the line name), then 4 to 6 topical ones.
5. Series idea: repeat one framing per product line so people recognise it (for example "Invisible: some embaixo da roupa").
6. Questions at the end ("qual cor você escolheria?") are common and cheap engagement. Use them on lifestyle days.
7. Not for nu.a: celebrity or creator names, seasonal costume themes, promo campaign names, luxury language.
